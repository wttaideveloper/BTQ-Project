# FaithIQ — How the App Works (Plain English Guide)

This guide explains the full user journey in simple words — no coding knowledge needed.

---

## What Is FaithIQ?

FaithIQ is a **Bible trivia game** you play in your web browser. You answer multiple-choice questions about the Bible, earn points, compete on leaderboards, challenge friends, and play **Team Battle** matches where two teams face off.

Think of it like a mix of:
- A solo quiz app (play alone anytime)
- A friend challenge app (play when it suits you both)
- A live game show (Team Battle with teammates)

---

## Step 1: Getting Started

### First visit

1. You open the website.
2. If you are **not logged in**, you are sent to the **Login / Register** page.
3. You can **create an account** (pick a username and password) or **log in** if you already have one.
4. After logging in, you land on the **Home page**.

### First-time welcome

- The first time you log in, a **short tutorial** pops up.
- It walks you through the main ways to play.
- You only see this once per account (you can still use the Help section later).

### Home page — your hub

From Home you can:

- Start a **solo game**
- Enter **Team Battle**
- Enter **Rapid Fire** (a faster Team Battle style)
- Open **Challenges** (friend vs friend)
- View the **Leaderboard**
- See **Game History**
- Check **Notifications** (bell icon)
- Log out

You need to be logged in for almost everything except the login page itself.

---

## Step 2: Playing Alone (Single Player)

This is the simplest path — just you and the questions.

### How you start

1. On Home, choose your settings:
   - **Category** — e.g. Bible Stories, Old Testament, New Testament
   - **Difficulty** — Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Game type:**
     - **Question mode** — Answer a set number of questions
     - **Time mode** — Answer as many as you can before time runs out
2. Tap **Play** (or open Game Setup and confirm).
3. You go to the **game screen**.

### During the game

1. A **question** appears with **four answer choices**.
2. You tap your answer.
3. You find out if you were **right or wrong**.
4. You may hear **sound effects** or **voice reading** the question (if turned on).
5. You move to the **next question** until the game ends.

### When it’s over

- You see your **final score** — how many you got right, time taken, etc.
- You might get a **reward popup** for doing well.
- Your score can be **saved** so it shows on the **Leaderboard** and in **Game History**.
- You go back to Home or play again.

---

## Step 3: Challenging a Friend (Async Challenge)

This is **not live at the same time**. You and a friend play the **same quiz separately**, and the app compares scores later.

### Sending a challenge

1. From Home, go to **Challenges**.
2. Pick a **friend** (another registered player).
3. Choose **category** and **difficulty**.
4. Send the challenge.

### What your friend sees

- They get a **notification** (bell on Home).
- They can **accept** or **decline**.
- If they accept, they play their round when ready.

### Playing your side

1. Open the challenge from **Challenges** or the notification.
2. Answer the same style of questions as solo play.
3. Finish your round.

### Getting results

- When **both players** have finished, the app picks a **winner** (or declares a **tie**).
- Both get a **notification** with the result.
- Challenges **expire** after about a day if not completed.

## Step 4: Team Battle (The Main Multiplayer Mode)

Team Battle is like a **live Bible quiz match** between **Team A** and **Team B**. Each team has a **captain** and **teammates**.

### Overview flow

```
Captain sets up a team
       ↓
Invite teammates + invite opposing captain
       ↓
Everyone joins and gets ready
       ↓
Both teams tap "Ready"
       ↓
Countdown → Game starts
       ↓
Toss question (who goes first?)
       ↓
10 questions — teams take turns
       ↓
Winner announced → Back to Home
```

---

### Phase A: Setting up your team

1. From Home, tap **Team Battle** (or **Rapid Fire** for the faster version).
2. You enter the **Team Battle setup** screen.
3. As **captain**, you:
   - Name your team
   - Pick category and difficulty
   - **Invite teammates** to join your side
   - **Invite an opponent captain** for the other team

### Phase B: Invitations

**If someone invites you:**

- You see a **notification** or invitation on setup.
- You can **accept** or **decline**.
- If you accept as **opponent captain**, you become captain of Team B and can invite your own teammates.
- If you accept as **teammate**, you join that captain’s team.

**While waiting:**

- You see who is **online** and **available**.
- The lobby shows **both teams** filling up.
- Captains can **remove** members or **leave** (which may cancel or reset the match).

### Phase C: Getting ready

1. When your team has the people you want, the **captain marks the team as Ready**.
2. The **other team** must also mark Ready.
3. When **both teams are ready**, a **countdown** starts.
4. Everyone is moved into the **Team Battle game screen**.

---

### Phase D: The toss (who goes first?)

Before the main quiz:

1. **One special question** is shown to **both teams**.
2. **Whichever team answers correctly first** wins the toss.
3. That team becomes **Team A** (goes first on odd questions).
4. The other team becomes **Team B**.

This is like a coin toss — it decides turn order for the rest of the match.

---

### Phase E: The main game (10 questions)

**Turn pattern:**

- **10 questions** total in a standard Team Battle.
- Teams **alternate** — one team’s turn, then the other’s.
- Team A typically answers questions 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.
- Team B typically answers questions 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.

**On your team’s turn:**

1. The **question** appears for everyone, but only the **answering team** submits.
2. **Each teammate** can pick their preferred answer (a suggestion).
3. The **captain** sees what teammates picked and chooses the **final answer** for the team.
4. The team gets points if the final answer is **correct**.

**On the other team’s turn:**

- You **watch and wait** (you can see the question but don’t submit for them).
- Scores update after each question.

**Extras you might notice:**

- **Sounds** and optional **voice** reading questions.
- A warning if you try to **leave the page** mid-game.
- Messages if someone **disconnects** or **leaves**.

---

### Phase F: End of Team Battle

1. After all questions, the app shows **final scores**.
2. The **winning team** is announced.
3. Stats are saved (correct answers, team scores, etc.).
4. Players return to **Home** or **Game History**.

**If someone leaves early:**

- The app may **end the match**, declare a **winner by forfeit**, or **reset the lobby** depending on who left (especially if a captain leaves).

---

## Step 5: Rapid Fire Team Battle

**Rapid Fire** is a **faster** Team Battle variant from Home.

- Same idea: two teams, live match, invitations, ready up.
- Questions and pacing are **quicker** — less waiting between turns.
- Good when you want a **shorter, snappier** team game.

The setup and invite flow feels similar to regular Team Battle; the difference is mainly **speed and intensity** during play.

---

## Step 6: Leaderboard & Game History

### Leaderboard

- Shows **top scores** from players across the app.
- You can compare how you rank against others.
- Filters may apply by **game type** or **category** depending on what you choose on that page.

### Game History

- A **personal log** of games you have played.
- Lets you look back at **past scores** and performance.
- Useful to track improvement over time.

---

## Step 7: Notifications

The **bell icon** on Home is your inbox inside the app.

You might get notified when:

- Someone **challenges** you
- A challenge is **accepted**, **declined**, or **finished**
- Someone **invites** you to a team or Team Battle
- A **join request** needs your approval (if you’re a captain)
- Challenge or battle **results** are ready

Tap a notification to go to the right place (challenge, team setup, etc.).

---

## Step 8: Admin Users (Special Role)

Some accounts are **admins**. After login they go to an **Admin Panel** instead of the normal Home-first experience.

Admins can:

- **Add, edit, and delete** trivia questions
- **Generate new questions** with AI help
- **Upload** batches of questions
- Manage **voice settings** (for question narration)
- View **question statistics**

Regular players never see this panel.

## Tips for New Players

1. **Log in first** — almost everything requires an account.
2. **Check the bell** — invitations and challenges live there.
3. **Team Battle needs people** — you need a captain on each side and at least one team ready on both sides.
4. **Captain has the final say** — in team turns, the captain picks the official answer.
5. **Don’t close the tab mid-game** — the app will warn you; leaving can hurt your team.
6. **Tutorial & FAQ** — on Home if you want a refresher without reading this doc.

---

## Summary in One Paragraph

You **sign up**, land on **Home**, and choose how to play: **alone**, **against a friend over time**, or **live as a team against another team**. Questions are multiple-choice Bible trivia with categories and difficulty levels. Solo play is immediate; challenges let two people play separately and compare; Team Battle is a structured live match with invites, a ready lobby, a toss for first turn, ten alternating team questions, and a winner at the end. Scores, history, and leaderboards help you track progress, and notifications keep you in the loop when friends invite or challenge you.
